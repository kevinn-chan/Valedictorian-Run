## Design principles for the revamp

1. **Spacing rhythm, not uniformity.** Vary section gaps: 48px between related sections, 80-96px between major sections. Currently everything is `py-10` or `py-12` with no variation.
2. **Visual hierarchy through weight, not just size.** Use font-weight, color contrast, and spacing to create hierarchy — not just bigger text.
3. **Navigation as architecture.** The session hub's 8 pill-links wrapping onto two rows needs to become a proper sidebar or tab bar with clear grouping.
4. **Cards only when they group.** Stop wrapping every list in `card-soft`. Use subtle dividers, spacing, and background tints instead. Cards should group related items, not wrap individual ones.
5. **Micro-interactions that confirm.** Flip animations on flashcards, slide transitions on grading, progress bar movement — feedback that says "something happened."
6. **Content-first.** The wiki reading experience and flashcard review are daily-use surfaces. They get the most attention.
